# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Spec OCR Pipeline is committed to a welcoming, technically rigorous community.
Participants must treat one another with respect, assume good faith, and make
room for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Positive participation includes:

- giving specific, constructive feedback;
- discussing ideas and evidence instead of attacking people;
- acknowledging mistakes and correcting them;
- respecting privacy, licensing, and the boundaries of other contributors;
- helping keep discussions focused and accessible.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized
attention, insults, deliberate disruption, doxxing, and publishing another
person's private information or credentials.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews,
and other project spaces. It also applies when someone publicly represents the
project.

## Reporting and Enforcement

Do not post sensitive conduct reports publicly. Use GitHub's reporting and
blocking tools and contact an active maintainer privately through a contact
method available on their GitHub profile.

Maintainers will review reports in good faith, protect the reporter's privacy
as far as practical, and respond proportionately. Responses may include a
private correction, a warning, temporary participation limits, removal of
content, or a permanent ban. Maintainers must recuse themselves when they are
personally involved in a report.

Retaliation against a person who reports a concern or participates in a review
is not tolerated.
